# HSPT Reading, Language and Verbal — content readiness and QA
Updated 2026-11-15

## Student-usable improvements committed on main

**Reading**
- All 20 learning days have a source-attributed, public-domain literary, historical or philosophical passage integrated into the graded timed Reading bank. Day 1 contains two; total 21 authentic excerpts.
- The first three timed papers have four 350+ word passages each (12 passages). At least 30 of 80 timed passages have been expanded to the advanced length target, and 50 still use shorter synthetic writing. Many changed question sets have individually written answer explanations.
- Every Reading day offers a separate **ten-question authentic-text challenge** selected from the passage already used in that day's full paper. It is untimed with immediate explanations, score, retakes, and persistent completion tracking. Because questions overlap the timed paper, it is a **review pathway, not an independent unseen assessment**.
- Source author, title, year and original public-domain text link display alongside sourced passages.
- All 20 preexisting 15-question guided-practice sessions and 20 timed 62-question papers remain available with their original IDs and saved progress.

**Language**
- At least 130 individually rewritten high-challenge questions integrated across the standard and advanced banks during the content upgrade: advanced multi-rule grammar, evidence evaluation, sampling bias, statistical inference and nuanced causation.
- Structural QA covers 20 daily sessions, 20 timed papers and advanced practice/challenges; duplicate detection includes questions within each session.
- Numerous cross-day templated cases remain and need separate editorial replacement.

**Verbal**
- Contextual synonym and antonym prompts use the studied word in a sentence; analogies include semantic opposition as well as near-synonymy.
- Advanced classification expanded to 65 curated semantic sets and selection randomized to reduce recycling across days/retakes.
- Fifty daily and fifty cumulative challenge configurations pass repeat generation tests of counts, answer keys and exact-question uniqueness.

**Functionality**
- Reading/Language scored submissions, refresh persistence, timer expiration, retakes, zero-score records and dashboard updates were checked with automated Chromium, Firefox and WebKit browser tests.
- The main overview provides direct access to advanced Reading challenges and counts saved challenge submissions; Reading challenge progress remains separate from full-paper and guided-practice status.
- Continuous integration and GitHub Pages results: https://github.com/qiprana/HSPTPREP/actions

## Remaining content acceptance blockers
- **50 of 80 conventional Reading timed passages** still fall below the advanced 350-word target; many accompanying 10-item sets use repeated evidence-role templates and require complete editorial rewriting. The authentic passages are not a substitute for upgrading the other three passages each day.
- Twenty existing Reading guided-practice passages remain short; 540 separate Reading vocabulary questions are not independently grade-10–12 calibrated.
- Language contains conceptual question repetition across different days; only a minority of its 2,060 question items have been individually reauthored during this upgrade.
- Verbal lexical distractor quality and difficulty consistency need independent blind review, especially synonym sense distinctions. Passing programmatic generators does not establish psychometric grade level.
- Independent student difficulty trials and actual macOS Safari / mobile-device checks are outstanding.

**Decision:** The enriched sections are **available for student practice and use**, with particularly strong authentic-text challenges and newly authored Language/Verbal cases. Do **not** advertise the complete question bank as uniformly advanced or declare full grade-10–12 pedagogical sign-off while the items above remain.
