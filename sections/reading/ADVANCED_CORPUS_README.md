# Authentic Reading excerpt corpus (Grades 10–11)

This folder holds **80 source-attributed excerpts from eight public-domain works** as proposed replacements for the existing synthetic Reading passages.

- `advanced-passages-v1.json`: 20 days × 4 authentic excerpts (source, author, year, word count, first/last text, provenance). **Editorial status: unreviewed; not live.** Excerpts are assembled at paragraph boundaries where possible and must be checked for coherent beginnings/endings and classroom suitability.
- `advanced-pilot-day01.json`: separately authored and reviewed demonstration for Day 1 when available; do not join excerpts with unrelated legacy questions.

These excerpts were selected from U.S.-public-domain texts in GITenberg copies of Project Gutenberg works. They are not newly composed fictional passages. Some historical language reflects its time and may need contextual notes. Copyright conditions may differ outside the United States.

The existing 62-question, 23-minute papers are not silently replaced. Grade 10–11 analytical questions require direct evidence, plausible distractors, clear explanations, an independent review, and a more appropriate pacing model.
